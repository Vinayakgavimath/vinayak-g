What I delivered

I designed a complete mobile task app from scratch. User flow, wireframes, design system, and a clickable Figma prototype. But what I'm more proud of is the thinking behind it. I started with the wrong solution and research corrected me. The final product is simpler than my first draft because I kept removing things that didn't serve the core idea.
