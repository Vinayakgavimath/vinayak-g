How I work
Process, not just pixels
01

Start with behavior, not assumptions

Before I open Figma, I want to know how users actually work. Real workflows always expose a different problem than the one we assumed.

02

Subtract before you add

The biggest UX wins I've had weren't adding features. They were removing steps, screens, and options that were in the user's way.

03

Hierarchy is a product decision

What you surface first is telling the user what matters. That's not a visual choice. I treat it like one of the most important design decisions on the screen.

About me

Designing clarity into messy workflows.

I'm a BCA student at Manipal University Jaipur, based in Bangalore. My focus is SaaS and B2B products, specifically the ones where the workflow is tangled and users are frustrated.

I got into UX because I kept noticing products that were technically functional but genuinely painful to use. My job, the way I see it, is to find where the friction is and remove it.

I'm currently looking for a UX internship where I can work on real problems and grow fast. If you're building something and want an extra set of eyes, reach out.
